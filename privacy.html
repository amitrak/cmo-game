Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 April 2025

The CMO Game (cmogame.com) is a free, browser-based marketing strategy game. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws.

We keep data collection to an absolute minimum. We do not use cookies. We do not track you across other websites. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

1. Who We Are

The CMO Game is operated as an independent project. If you have any privacy questions or requests, contact us at: privacy@cmogame.com

For the purposes of GDPR, the operator of cmogame.com is the data controller.

2. What Data We Collect and Why

We only collect data when you voluntarily choose to submit your score to the public leaderboard. Playing the game without submitting a score generates no personal data on our end.

Anonymous gameplay events

When a game ends (win or loss), we record an anonymous event containing non-personal game statistics (product type, positioning, final revenue, brand equity, month reached). This data contains no name or identifier and is used solely to understand how the game is being played so we can improve it.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Leaderboard data: We process your player name and score on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you tick the checkbox and click "Submit to Hall of Fame." Consent is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Section 6).
  • Anonymous gameplay events: We process non-personal game statistics on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding gameplay patterns to improve the game. No personal data is involved.

4. How We Store Your Data

Leaderboard data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database, operated by Google LLC. Firebase acts as our data processor.

Google's data centres are primarily located in the United States. By submitting your score, you acknowledge that your player name and score will be transferred to and stored in the US. This transfer is covered by Google's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and Google's Data Processing Terms, which comply with GDPR requirements for international data transfers.

For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Leaderboard entries: Retained indefinitely unless you request deletion. The leaderboard displays the top 100 scores; entries outside the top 100 are not publicly shown but may remain stored.
  • Anonymous gameplay events: Retained for up to 12 months for analytics purposes, then deleted.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with applicable privacy laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Ask us to delete your leaderboard entry at any time.
  • Right to restriction: Ask us to restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent: You can withdraw your consent for leaderboard processing at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@cmogame.com with the subject line "Privacy Request" and include the player name and approximate date of your submission so we can locate your entry. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK, or your national supervisory authority in the EU).

7. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies of any kind — not session cookies, not analytics cookies, not advertising cookies.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking scripts. The only third-party scripts loaded are:

  • Google Fonts (for the game's typefaces)
  • Firebase SDK (for the leaderboard database)
  • Chart.js (for in-game revenue charts)
  • canvas-confetti (for celebration animations)
  • html2canvas (for the score screenshot feature)

Note: Google Fonts requests your browser's IP address to serve font files. This is handled by Google under their own privacy policy. We do not receive or store this data.

8. Children's Privacy

The CMO Game is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted data to our leaderboard, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
